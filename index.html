About

Background & Motivation

I am Derick Nganyu Tanyu, a researcher and technologist whose career spans telecommunications engineering, advanced mathematics, scientific computing, and applied AI. My work sits at the intersection of rigorous mathematical theory and practical technological impact — a space I have navigated across three countries and multiple disciplines.

My journey began in engineering: I studied Telecommunications Engineering at the University of Yaounde, Cameroon, and went on to work as a Consultant and Datacom Engineer at Huawei Technologies, building and optimizing complex communication infrastructure. This grounding in engineering gave me a deep appreciation for systems that must work reliably in the real world.

Drawn by the power of mathematics to describe and solve hard problems, I pursued an MSc in Mathematical Sciences at AIMS Ghana and then a PhD in Industrial Mathematics at the University of Bremen, Germany. My doctoral and postdoctoral research at ZeTeM (Centre for Industrial Mathematics) focused on scientific machine learning for inverse problems — learning how to reconstruct hidden quantities from indirect, noisy measurements, with applications in medical imaging and engineering.

My research introduced and explored deep learning approaches to classical problems in applied mathematics: how can neural networks learn the structure of physical systems governed by partial differential equations? How can we use data efficiently when we also know the physics? These questions led me to work on physics-informed neural networks, Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT), parameter identification, and regularization theory for ill-posed inverse problems.

Since January 2025, I have been a Developer at SAP in the Private Cloud organization, where I work within the SEO AI Expert & Enablement Core Team. My focus is on AI experiments, technical enablement, and identifying cross-function efficiency opportunities using modern AI tools and automation. I am applying the same scientific rigor and curiosity I developed in research to the challenges of enterprise-scale AI.

What drives me is the belief that mathematics and AI, when developed and deployed thoughtfully, can transform how we understand and interact with complex systems — from the human body to industrial processes to large organizations. I am always interested in conversations about research, applied AI, and the science-industry interface.
